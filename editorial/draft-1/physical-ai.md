# Physical AI
## What happens when intelligence moves?

Software systems operate in a world where requests can often be retried. A machine acting in the physical world does not always have that luxury. Its environment changes continuously. Delay can become a correctness or safety problem rather than merely a performance problem.

Physical AI brings intelligence into perception, motion and action. It combines sensing, state estimation, planning, control and interaction with other physical actors. The advances in models and robotics are fascinating, but I am particularly interested in what happens when these systems stop being isolated machines.

Imagine several robots sharing a workspace, or a human and machine manipulating the same object. They need more than an exchange of position coordinates. They need sufficiently aligned views of the world, coordinated intentions, predictable behavior and mechanisms for handling uncertainty. The meaning of 'shared state' now includes time, physical constraints and the consequences of action.

Yet we should not assume every such problem requires a new network. Local control loops, shared models, edge systems and existing industrial communications already solve important pieces. The open systems question is where coordination must occur, what state must be shared, how fresh that state must be, and which actions remain locally autonomous.

This frontier is less a claim about a finished architecture than an invitation to examine it. As embodied systems become more capable, it becomes harder to separate intelligence from the physical systems that sense and enact it.

### Questions worth pursuing
- What world state should autonomous machines share, and at what fidelity?
- How do we coordinate actions safely when observations arrive late or disagree?
- Where should autonomy be local, and where should collective behavior be negotiated?
- How do we verify physical outcomes rather than only software intentions?
- Can heterogeneous robots form coherent teams without requiring one central controller?
